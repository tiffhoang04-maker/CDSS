# Minimum Initial Assessment

## Immediate threats
- Airway status
- Respiratory effort
- Circulation and major bleeding
- Level of consciousness
- Immediate environmental danger

## Vital signs
- Heart rate
- Blood pressure
- Respiratory rate
- Oxygen saturation
- Temperature
- Pain score

## Clinical context
- Chief concern
- Symptom onset
- Mechanism or precipitating event
- Relevant history
- Medications
- Allergies
- Available medical resources
